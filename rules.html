Rules or the halloween quiz

1. There are 15 questions. You must answer all the questions.

2. There is no going back.

3. You shouldn't use any external resource to answer questions.

4. On every wrong answer, you will lose a point and you will get two options to choose from. First option is to end the game right there and second one is to see a jumpscare to continue your game. Sounds scary, right?

5. As the question will move on, the jumpscares will become scarier.

6. You can quit the quiz anytime you want. To quit the game enter 'quit'.

7. Have fun and don't be scared!
